    What's indexed. Everything these open sources list for each UTC day: new arXiv submissions, bioRxiv and medRxiv first versions, PhilArchive and PhilSci-Archive additions, IACR ePrint, HAL theses, reports and preprints, Zenodo publications, and Crossref, DataCite and OpenAlex records typed as preprints, reports or working papers, and dissertations. The same work found in more than one place is listed once, with the other places noted. Crossref and OpenAlex often get records days late, so each day is collected again three days later.
